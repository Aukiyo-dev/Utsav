Why is Durga Puja celebrated?

Durga Puja is a major festival dedicated to Goddess Durga. It is celebrated with worship, cultural programmes, artistic pandals, community gatherings and the immersion of the Durga image at the end of the festival. In West Bengal, the festival is especially known for its elaborate public celebrations and distinctive artistic installations.
