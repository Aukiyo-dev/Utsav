What is Kali Puja?

Kali Puja is a Hindu festival dedicated to Goddess Kali. In West Bengal and other parts of eastern India, the festival is an important part of the Diwali-period celebrations. Homes, temples and community spaces may be decorated with lamps and other festive arrangements, while devotees perform worship during the night.
