When is Mahalaya 2026?

Mahalaya 2026 is on Saturday, 10 October 2026. Mahalaya is traditionally associated with the beginning of the Durga Puja season in Bengali culture and with the early-morning Mahishasura Mardini broadcast.

What is Mahalaya?

Mahalaya is an important Bengali observance marking the traditional start of the festive season leading into Durga Puja. Many people associate the morning with devotional music, recitation and the familiar Mahishasura Mardini broadcast.
